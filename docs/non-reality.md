# Synthetic Users are not real users

u-sekai produces **simulated** participants. The simulated participants
do not have:

- subjective experience,
- embodiment,
- attention or fatigue in any human sense,
- accessibility needs unless explicitly modelled by the
  capability profile (and 0.1.0 does not model those).

Self-reports, observer findings, and behavioural evidence are
**exploratory signals**, not measurements of human behaviour.

Treat u-sekai outputs the same way you would treat a usability study
run on a small convenience sample: useful for hypothesis generation,
spike detection, and pre-merge sanity checks; **not** valid as a
substitute for usability research with real participants.

Real-user calibration, generative benchmarks, and universal UX scores
are explicitly deferred (see [`docs/research-issues/`](./research-issues/)).
